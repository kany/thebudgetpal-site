Support

Need help with Budget Pal? Email support@thebudgetpal.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password" and enter the email you used to sign up. We'll send you a reset link.

I'm not getting verification or reset emails

Check your spam folder. If it's still missing after a few minutes, email support@thebudgetpal.com and we'll help.

How do I delete my account?

Email support@thebudgetpal.com from the address on the account and we'll delete your account and all associated data. In-app account deletion is planned.

Found a bug or have a feature request?

Email support@thebudgetpal.com with as much detail as you can — what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. Screenshots help.
